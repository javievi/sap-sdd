## 2. Business objective and scope

### Objective and users

Developers preparing for OPC downtime need a read-only list of periodic jobs potentially affected by a single-date downtime and configurable before/after buffers. Trigger: execute the assessment with impact date/start/end and durations. Success target: estimated 90% fewer manual steps, evaluated by human comparison; no automated benefit measurement or runtime SLA is required. OUC is the sole research target; OPC business context does not authorize OPC access.

### In scope

- Forecast released periodic candidates with blank EVENTID and stored scheduled date on/before impact date, including old seeds and calendar-controlled recurrence.
- Evaluate minute/hour/day/week/month schedules over full date-time assessment bounds, including adjacent dates.
- Include impact and after-buffer starts without duration evidence; before-buffer overlap requires maximum usable same-name history, with missing-history policy still blocked.
- Consolidate by candidate identity, show extracted scheduling context and inclusion evidence in an ALV Grid.
- Describe concrete changes to the existing selected report while recording unresolved ALM/selected/historical program-name conflict.

### Out of scope

- Job writes, execution, cancellation, release/rescheduling, scheduler modification, persistence, cross-system access, deployment or release workflows.
- Broadening candidate scheduled-date filter to assessment-end date. A first-scheduled next-day job remains excluded even when the after-buffer crosses into that date; occurrences from an eligible earlier seed can qualify then.
- Inferring future starts for nonperiodic/event-triggered candidates or adding an implicit predecessor exclusion to FR-04.
- Carrying forward R010's materialized-only limitation as fulfillment of forecasting; silently skipping unsupported calendars or unknown duration; implementing during this portal request.

Compatibility boundaries: existing PA_DATE/PA_TIMFR/PA_TIMTO names and known job identity fields are reusable. Buffer representation, optional SO_JOBNM behavior and saved selection variants need explicit compatibility review. Old R010 ZISCT and current selected ZISHM are not assumed aliases or synchronized copies.

Ordered processing (proposed design, not executable code): validate date/times/durations; resolve zone and all four bounds; establish authorized read context and one history observation cutoff T0; retrieve/filter/deduplicate candidate headers; forecast candidate occurrences using verified scheduler semantics; classify each forecast start by BR-004; fetch/cache complete usable history by exact name when a before-buffer occurrence needs it; calculate maximum and checked estimated finish; consolidate qualifying occurrences by candidate key and chosen representative; display results with scope and completeness context.

Evaluate impact interval before after-buffer so S=I1 is 'impact window'. Before-buffer membership excludes I0 because I0 belongs to impact. A0 is inclusive but still requires overlap if A0 precedes I0; being at A0 alone is insufficient. Zero buffers create no separate before/after interval; equal impact times create one included instant. A forecast before A0 does not qualify even if its estimated finish reaches impact. Do not stop at an early nonqualifying occurrence if a later one qualifies.

Duplicate source rows/forecast instants cannot multiply result rows. Missing/invalid scheduled date, nonadvancing recurrence, unsupported control value and unavailable calendar must not be interpreted as 'no qualifying jobs'. Details of before-only missing history and incomplete candidate schedules are blockers, not assumed user decisions. Historical intervals use complete dates across midnight/month/year; negative or incomplete intervals are unusable. A valid zero-second interval is distinct from absence, but cannot make a start strictly before I0 overlap.

Worked example: history durations 1800, 3600, 5400 seconds yield maximum 5400. Forecast 08:30 before a 10:00 impact qualifies exactly at 10:00; 08:29:59 finishes at 09:59:59 and does not. A later 10:15 forecast of that same candidate qualifies independently. An eligible prior-day seed can forecast next-day 00:15 inside an after-buffer; a different candidate first scheduled on that next day fails the unchanged FR-04 date predicate.
