## 2. Business objective and scope

### Objective and users

Enable the 10014 report user to have system adjustment accounts automatically represented beneath the applicable configured GL account and included by subsequent 10014 loading. E-001 identifies the actor only as User; operator role, job owner and mapping trigger are unknown. Success means a corresponding parent-child association from maintained translation and inclusion of the child's qualifying data, without changing unrelated report behavior.

### In scope

- Map the corresponding adjustment account from ZFI_CONF_PARA beneath the applicable GL account in ZGFGLACCT (BR-001).
- Make 10014 loading consume the updated association (BR-002).
- Specify proposed idempotency, isolation, diagnostics and regression safeguards (BR-003 to BR-005), with their undecided contracts visible.
- Refresh R001 evidence and preserve every existing BR, AC, plan and test ID in the R002 normative design.

### Out of scope

- ABAP implementation, configuration/SAP/ALM changes, activation, report execution, tests and implementer handoff in this session.
- New UI, RAP, OData, CDS conversion or translation-maintenance redesign.
- Changes to financial calculations, rounding, currencies/units, fiscal periods, selection semantics or output schemas except the required account inclusion; any necessary coupled change needs evidence and approved scope.
- Historical backfill, stale-mapping deletion/reparenting and cross-client propagation without an explicit decision. Existing stale/conflicting data must be addressed by policy before implementation, not silently deleted.

### AC-006 - Authorization and operational failures do not produce false success

- Rules: BR-003, BR-005; proposed integrity/regression criterion blocked by B-002/B-005.
- Given separately an unauthorized caller and an authorized caller encountering a dependency or loader failure; include lock/write failures and concurrent identical attempts only if persistent mapping is selected.
